Logo Concept

The icon represents knowledge compounding over time through three stacked layers:

• Bottom layer (lightest): Foundation knowledge
• Middle layer (medium): Accumulated understanding
• Top layer (darkest): Current, active work

The vertical connection lines suggest addressability and linkage between layers—reflecting Compound's document-first, reference-based architecture.
